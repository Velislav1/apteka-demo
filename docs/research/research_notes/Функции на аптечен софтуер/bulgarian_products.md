# Pharmacy management software sold in Bulgaria: feature inventory by product

Method note: WebFetch could not reach any vendor site from this session (DNS blocked for phoenixpharma.bg, alcorsoft.bg, click-bg.com). Every finding below comes from web search result summaries and snippets, not from reading the full pages. Treat feature lists as vendor claims seen in snippets. ## Products and their feature sets

### Takeaway
The Bulgarian market looks concentrated. Phoenix Pharma's „Аптека Експерт 2“ (Pharmacy Expert, sold under its "Здравен софтуер" unit) is the clear leader. A tail of smaller vendors follows: Alcor Soft „Фарма Про“, КЛИК „CLICK Drug“, AS Systems „PharmaStar“/PharmaCloud, SIS Technology „POS Master“ and Sopharmacy's in-house SAP system. Generic trade software (Microinvest Склад Pro, USU) is also used. I found no evidence that „Сеневир“, „Апис“, „Вивалди“, „ФармаСис“, „ПроФарма“ or „Аптека+“ exist as current Bulgarian pharmacy products. I could not confirm M-Soft (Харманли) as the vendor of Аптека Експерт 2: the current owner and seller is Phoenix Pharma.

**Regulatory context that shapes every product**
- НЗОК publishes requirements for pharmacy software (since 16.11.2012 programs must match them), XML report formats for pharmacy claims (current version in force from 01.09.2024), and twice-monthly "Update Pharma" nomenclature update files. The 2026 page lists packages from March to July 2026. — [nhif.bg software/drugstores](https://www.nhif.bg/bg/software/drugstores); [2026 updates](https://www.nhif.bg/bg/software/drugstores/updates/2026); [2012 requirements doc](https://www.nhif.bg/upload/12501/software-apteki-iziskvanija-16-11-2012.doc)
- E-prescriptions: before filling, the pharmacy software must check the prescription through an НЗОК service and obtain an НРН for the dispensing from НЗИС, accessed with a pharmacist's КЕП. Software can also retrieve the history of issued and filled prescriptions. Since 16.10.2023, diabetes drugs and systemic antibacterials are prescribed only electronically. — [NHIF instructions (search summary)](https://www.nhif.bg/upload/12501/software-apteki-iziskvanija-16-11-2012.doc); [strategy.bg consultation 8417](https://www.strategy.bg/bg/public-consultations/8417/export)
- Cost benchmark (old, 2015 БФС presentation): about 840 BGN per workstation for pharmacy software, plus about 650 BGN for hardware and licensed OS. — [BPhU presentation 2015](https://bphu.bg/upload/files/Presentation_Svetoslav%20Krumov_BPhU_19022015.pdf)

## Table stakes vs differentiators

### Takeaway
Every specialised program shares a compliance baseline: НЗОК reporting and nomenclature updates, НЗИС e-prescription, BgMVO verification, fiscal device or СУПТО, plus batches and expiry dates and barcode sales. Differentiators are forecast-based automatic ordering, central chain management, accounting and web-shop export, card-terminal integration, price comparison and cloud delivery.

- НЗОК, e-prescription and verification appear in every specialised product found: Аптека Експерт ([Phoenix](https://www.phoenixpharma.bg/en/pharmacy-expert/)), Фарма Про ([Alcor](https://alcorsoft.bg/products/pharma-pro)), CLICK Drug ([КЛИК](https://www.click-bg.com/products/drug)), and the BgMVO-tested list ([BgMVO](https://platform.bgmvo.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/%D0%98%D0%A2-%D0%B4%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B0%D0%B2%D1%87%D0%B8%D1%86%D0%B8-%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B4%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B3%D0%B0%D1%89%D0%B8-%D1%81%D0%BE%D1%84%D1%82%D1%83%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%BD%D0%B8-%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%88%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%8F-%D0%B7%D0%B0-%D0%B2%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D1%84%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%B0%D1%86%D0%B8%D1%8F-%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BA%D0%B0%D1%80%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B0-%D0%B2-%D0%91%D1%8A%D0%BB%D0%B3%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B8%D1%8F.pdf)).

## What pharmacists complain about or praise

### Takeaway
Public complaints are mostly about the regulatory IT chain (НЗИС outages, NHIF non-payment despite an НРН) and the burden of constant mandated software changes, plus the 2025–2026 euro changeover. I found no product-specific reviews.

### Cited Findings
- An all-day outage of e-prescription systems in pharmacies: some pharmacies lost the signal, and НЗИС returned invalid reference numbers. — [Cross.bg](https://www.cross.bg/nzis-retzepti-patzientite-1764607.html); [Dnes.bg](https://www.dnes.bg/a/5-zdrave/669282-sriv-v-e-retseptite-patsienti-ostanaha-bez-lekarstva); [24zdrave.bg](https://www.24zdrave.bg/article/19897085)
- Changes to pharmacy software from 1 April 2025: the pharmacists' initiative committee said fixes belong in НЗИС, not in pharmacy software, and that pharmacy software is "вече претоварен с доработки" with every change risking outages. They also say prescriptions with an НРН sometimes stay unpaid by НЗОК. — [glasnews.bg](https://glasnews.bg/zdrave/farmatsevti-promenite-aptechniia-softuer-1-april-shte-561471/); [24plovdiv.bg](https://www.24plovdiv.bg/zdrave/article/19869397); [pbnovini.com (2025)](https://pbnovini.com/balgariya/2025/farmatsevti-promenite-v-aptechniya-softuer-ot-1-april-shte-zatrudnyat-dostapa-na-patsientite-do-lekarstva)
- Euro changeover:
  - Pharmacists complained that their software was not updated in time and that cash registers were not ready.
  - In some software the sale screen still showed the lev price, with the euro price reachable only through extra steps.
  - One pharmacy owner wrote his own software instead.
  - Dual pricing must be on every package in advance, and fines for labelling errors run up to 5,000/7,000 лв.
  
  — [24plovdiv.bg](https://www.24plovdiv.bg/amp/20943538); [dnesplus.bg](https://dnesplus.bg/bulgaria/aptekite-imat-problemi-s-dvoynoto-etiketirane-na-tsenite-na-lekarstvata_1198011)
- Dual display ended 8 August 2026: from 9 August 2026 prices are shown in euro only. An older pharmacy-sector source gave 31.12.2026, from before the date was fixed. — [dataplus-bg.com](https://www.dataplus-bg.com/kray-na-dvojnoto-oboznachavane/)
- Praise and adoption signal: 1,100+ pharmacists attended the Аптека Експерт 2 euro-adaptation webinar. — [LinkedIn](https://bg.linkedin.com/in/peyo-angelov)

### Inferences
- Pharmacists value vendors who ship regulatory changes on time and reliably. Outages in the e-prescription chain stop work at the counter, so offline tolerance and clear error messages matter.
- Our demo already uses EUR with Bulgarian formatting, which matches the post-August-2026 state.

## Electronic data exchange with wholesalers

### Takeaway
E-orders exist in practice: Sopharma Trading has an „е-заявка“ entry point, Phoenix's own software places forecast-based supplier orders, and Sting supplies 3,345 pharmacies. Sopharmacy and Sopharma Trading use EDI (Comarch EDI through Eltrade) for orders, delivery notes, invoices and order confirmations. CLICK Drug imports electronic invoice files. I found no public, standard Bulgarian file format for importing an electronic delivery.

### Cited Findings
- Sopharma Trading's site has an „е-заявка“ button. The company reaches over 3,000 pharmacies and 350+ hospitals. — [sopharmatrading.com](https://www.sopharmatrading.com/?lang=bg)
- Comarch EDI (sold by Eltrade) carries orders, stock receipts (стокови разписки), invoices and order confirmations. Софармаси exchanges data with suppliers through EDI, and Sopharma Trading is listed among the partners. — [Eltrade EDI](https://www.eltrade.com/bg/elektronen-obmen-na-danni/); [Eltrade Софармаси](https://www.eltrade.com/bg/sofarmasi/)
- In Serbia, Sopharma Trading launched a B2B portal (on Hybris) for orders, delivery tracking and invoices. That is not confirmed for Bulgaria. — [economic.bg](https://www.economic.bg/bg/a/view/sofarma-trejding-ad-obedinyava-operatsiite-si-v-sarbiya-pod-korporativniya-si-brand-101461)
- Sting AD: 448 suppliers and 3,345 pharmacies, full order execution with free delivery to the pharmacy. — [stingpharma.com logistics](https://www.stingpharma.com/en/logistics-and-distribution/)
- The wholesaler Pharmnet commissioned microservices to sync prices and stock from Sting, Sopharma and Phoenix, which shows that wholesaler data feeds exist. — [Wollow Soft portfolio](https://wollow-soft.com/en-US/Portfolio/66/building-microservices-for-a-pharmaceutical-company)
- CLICK Drug: data import from electronic invoice files. — [click-bg.com](https://www.click-bg.com/products/drug)
- Pharmacy Expert: automatic supplier orders from forecasting models. — [Phoenix](https://www.phoenixpharma.bg/en/pharmacy-expert/)

### Inferences
- Exchange is per wholesaler (proprietary feeds or EDI) rather than a national standard. For our demo, a future „импорт на електронна доставка“ would need one adapter per supplier. That belongs in `docs/BACKLOG.md`, not in the demo.

### Gaps
- I found no documented file format (XML/CSV fields) for Phoenix, Sting or Sopharma electronic deliveries, nor details of how Аптека Експерт imports them.
- Not confirmed whether Bulgarian wholesalers send structured e-invoices (e.g. UBL) to pharmacies.
